Recommendation type: Video / YouTube

Summary:

Walk through why Shopify apparel brands struggle with model product photography: cost, logistics, slow turnaround, inconsistent styling, limited model coverage, and hard-to-repeat looks. Then demo a repeatable Tiny Lemon workflow that turns a single flat-lay into consistent on-model product images for Shopify product pages. End with a quick checklist for photo consistency that improves product-page trust and fit understanding.

Suggested video structure:

1. Problem: why model photography slows small Shopify apparel teams down.
2. Input: start with one flat-lay or catalog product photo.
3. Workflow: generate on-model product images in Tiny Lemon.

5. Shopify handoff: show or explain publishing back to product listings.
6. Checklist: lighting, garment visibility, consistent crops, variation coverage, product-page placement.
