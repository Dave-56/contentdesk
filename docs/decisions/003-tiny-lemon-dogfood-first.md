# 003 — Dogfood Tiny Lemon first

**Decision:** First real use of the visibility layer is on **Tiny Lemon**, our own Shopify
app, on **Perplexity only**, over a 30–60 day window. Prove ContentDesk lifts Tiny Lemon's
AI-search citations before selling the loop externally or adding more providers.

**Why:** Need proof the loop changes outcomes, not just produces scores. Our own app is the
safest, fastest, lowest-stakes test bed and gives us real operator language for sales.

**Revisit when:** Tiny Lemon shows measurable citation lift (or a clear null result) at the
end of the experiment window — then decide on multi-provider and external customers.
