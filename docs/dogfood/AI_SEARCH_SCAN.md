## Key Pattern: Competitors Are Weaponizing Listicles

Most of the top-ranking listicles are written *by competitors* (WearView, Claid, Photoroom, Photta, Veeton, Rewarx, Rawshot, Style3D). They rank themselves first and include some competitors as filler. This is the dominant AEO move in the category — ranking listicles double as both citation surfaces *and* lead generation.

Implication for Tiny Lemon:

```text
1. Outreach to be included in competitor-owned listicles (works but caps placement).
2. Build a Tiny Lemon-owned listicle (more upside, more honest tradeoffs).
3. Both. The competitors do both.
```

## App Store Listing Language Gap

Tiny Lemon's homepage uses: *"Studio shots from flat-lays in 60 seconds."*

AI engines and buyer prompts use:

```text
on-model photos
AI fashion photography
Botika alternative
flatlay to model

```

The App Store listing and homepage should incorporate this language directly. Without it, the engines have less reason to surface Tiny Lemon for the buyer prompts that matter.
