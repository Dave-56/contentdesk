# ContentDesk Pricing Spec

## Market Positioning

The market is crowded, but it does not have one obvious winner yet. It has buckets:

- **HubSpot AEO**: likely the strongest mainstream SMB threat because it combines tracking, recommendations, and HubSpot execution. It says plans start around **$50/month**. Source: [HubSpot](https://www.hubspot.com/products/aeo/best-aeo-software)
- **Profound**: more enterprise, agency, and serious AI visibility platform. Often positioned as a premium leader, with public comparisons showing higher pricing. Source: [SolCrys pricing comparison](https://solcrys.com/aeo-platform-pricing-2026/)
- **Otterly / Peec / RankPrompt / others**: cheaper or mid-market prompt/citation tracking tools.
- **Semrush / Ahrefs / SEO suites**: adding AI visibility onto existing SEO workflows.

ContentDesk should not be built as another AEO dashboard. That lane is getting brutal.

The sharper ContentDesk wedge:

```text
Give me one high-confidence page to publish next, based on prompt gaps.
```

That is different from:

```text
Here is your AI visibility dashboard.
```

For a lean founder, the pain is not only:

```text
Am I cited?
```

The pain is:

```text
Okay, I am missing. What do I publish now?
```

That is where ContentDesk can still be interesting.

## Pricing Principle

Do not start as a cheap all-you-can-eat SaaS.

That is dangerous because the work is too service-heavy. If ContentDesk charges $19-$49/month while doing deep research, drafting, QA, recommendations, and recheck planning, it risks pricing itself out of the business.

The key is:

```text
Do not sell AI visibility tracking.
Sell the done-for-you decision and asset.
```

## Pricing Layers

### 1. Free / Lead Magnet

Offer:

```text
Drop your site, get one page recommendation.
```

This is what already worked on Reddit.

Purpose:

- Validate demand.
- Capture founder pain.
- Identify whether the founder wants the full brief, draft, or recurring workflow.
- Show value without asking for keywords, competitors, analytics access, or a long form.

### 2. Paid One-Off Kit: $49-$149

Offer:

```text
One publish-ready SEO/AEO page kit.
```

Includes:

- prompt gap
- recommended page angle
- outline
- draft
- FAQ
- metadata
- publish checklist
- recheck prompts

This is the first paid validation step.

The question it answers:

```text
After seeing the gap, will founders pay to get the asset created?
```

### 3. Recurring: $199-$499/month

Offer:

```text
One or two content kits per month plus recheck.
```

This is for founders who already believe in content and want consistency.

The recurring promise should not be:

```text
Track your AI citations.
```

Better recurring promise:

```text
Every month, ContentDesk tells you the highest-leverage page to publish next and gives you the kit to ship it.
```

## Positioning Summary

ContentDesk can be a profitable business if it avoids the crowded dashboard fight and starts almost like a productized strategist.

Bad positioning:

```text
Track your AI citations.
```

Better positioning:

```text
Every month, ContentDesk tells you the highest-leverage page to publish next and gives you the kit to ship it.
```

Best early wedge:

```text
From prompt gap to publish-ready page.
```

That is narrower, more useful, and easier to charge for.
