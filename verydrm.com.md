## Product / Category Understanding

VeryDRM helps businesses protect digital content after it has been shared. The product sits at the intersection of PDF DRM, secure document sharing, video/audio content protection, virtual data rooms, and enterprise file access control.

The homepage leads with controlling access after sharing: encrypting, tracking, and sharing sensitive files while blocking unauthorized downloading, copying, printing, screenshots, and screen recording. It supports PDFs, Office files, images, video, audio, HTML/web content, secure links, virtual data rooms, dynamic watermarks, access expiry, view limits, device/IP controls, revocation, analytics, and API/integration workflows.

The blog titles show that VeryDRM is already aiming at comparison and buyer-evaluation intent, especially against Vitrium, Digify, and Locklizard, plus broad "best DRM file protection software" and "how to protect PDFs" searches.

## Category

VeryDRM appears to compete in:

**DRM file protection and secure content sharing software for PDFs, media, and business documents.**

Alternative labels:

- PDF DRM protection software
- Enterprise document security software
- Secure file sharing with DRM controls
- Digital rights management software for business files
- Secure virtual data room with DRM controls
- Content protection software for online courses, eBooks, PDFs, and videos
- Anti-copy / anti-download / anti-screenshot document protection software

The key category insight is that VeryDRM should not be positioned only as a PDF password or file-sharing tool. The stronger buyer-facing category is persistent DRM and access-control software for businesses that need to keep control after documents or media are sent outside their own environment.

## Primary Buyer

Primary buyer:

**Businesses that sell, share, or distribute valuable digital content and need to prevent unauthorized copying, downloading, forwarding, printing, screenshots, or redistribution.**

Related buyer segments:

- Online course creators and training companies protecting videos, study guides, and paid course materials
- eBook publishers, authors, research publishers, and digital content sellers protecting PDF downloads
- Corporate training and HR teams sharing internal manuals, policies, or onboarding materials
- Legal, finance, accounting, consulting, and healthcare teams sharing confidential client or patient documents
- M&A advisors, private equity firms, startups, and fundraising teams sharing data room materials
- Real estate, architecture, manufacturing, and technical teams sharing plans, drawings, contracts, reports, or CAD-adjacent files
- Developers or platforms that need DRM protection through APIs, LMS, CMS, ecommerce, CRM, Google Drive, OneDrive, Gmail, Outlook, or intranet integrations

These segments overlap, but the job-to-be-done is the important part: the buyer needs to share digital content without losing control of who can view it, copy it, print it, download it, screenshot it, or forward it.

## Product Job

The buyer is trying to:

**Share valuable PDFs, videos, documents, or other digital files while keeping persistent control over access, copying, downloading, printing, screenshots, and redistribution.**

More specifically, they want to:

- Protect PDFs from copying, downloading, printing, screenshots, and unauthorized sharing
- Add dynamic user-identifiable watermarks to discourage leaks and trace redistribution
- Revoke access after a file has already been shared
- Set expiry dates, view limits, print limits, and user/device/IP restrictions
- Track who opened files, when they opened them, and how they interacted with them
- Protect course videos, paid training materials, eBooks, reports, and sensitive business files
- Share confidential files through secure links or a virtual data room
- Integrate DRM controls into an LMS, CMS, ecommerce flow, CRM, cloud drive, email workflow, or custom platform
